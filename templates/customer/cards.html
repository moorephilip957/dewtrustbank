Frequently Asked Questions

What is a debit card?

A debit card is a payment card linked directly to your bank account. It can be used for online and in-store purchases, ATM withdrawals, and everyday transactions, allowing you to spend money available in your account securely and conveniently.

How secure are debit cards?

Debit cards come with advanced security features such as PIN protection, EMV chip technology, transaction alerts, and fraud monitoring to help keep your money and account information safe during online and in-store transactions.

Can I have multiple debit cards?

Yes, many we allow you to request for multiple debit cards for different accounts or authorized users. Each card can be managed with its own spending controls and account settings.

How long does it take to get a debit card?

Debit cards are usually issued within a few business days after your account is approved. Some banks may also provide instant debit card access or temporary cards while you wait for the physical card to arrive.
