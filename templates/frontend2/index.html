Our Mobile Deposit allows you to deposit a check through the Havin Savings mobile app using your internet-enabled iPhone® or Android™ mobile device, provided your device has a camera. You must be an Online or Mobile banking customer, and enrolled in the Havin Savings Mobile Deposit service. In the Havin Savings mobile app, select "Mobile Deposit," then follow the steps to enroll or deposit a check.
