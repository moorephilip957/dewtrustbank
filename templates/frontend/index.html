How do I register for mobile banking at First Havin Bank?

  • If you are already enrolled in Online Banking, simply log in using your username and password through the First Havin Bank Savings app.
  • After logging in, Android® and iPhone® users can access mobile banking features instantly.
  • You may also enroll in Mobile Deposit to deposit checks using your mobile device camera.
  • To enroll, select “Mobile Deposit” from the main menu, then review and accept the terms.
  • If you are not yet registered for Online Banking, you can sign up online.

What is Mobile Deposit?

Mobile Deposit allows you to deposit checks using the First Havin Bank Savings mobile app on any internet-enabled iPhone® or Android™ device with a camera. You must be enrolled in Online or Mobile Banking and activated for the Mobile Deposit service. Simply select “Mobile Deposit” in the app and follow the on-screen instructions to complete your deposit.
