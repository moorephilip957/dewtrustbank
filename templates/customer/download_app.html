Manual Installation Guide

Android Installation

  1. 1 Open Chrome browser and visit this banking website
  2. 2 Tap the menu (three dots) in the top-right corner
  3. 3 Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
  4. 4 Tap "Add" to install the app on your home screen

iOS Installation

  1. 1 Open Safari browser and visit this banking website
  2. 2 Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up)
  3. 3 Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. 4 Tap "Add" to install the app on your home screen

Desktop Installation

  1. 1 Open Chrome, Edge, or Firefox browser
  2. 2 Visit this banking website
  3. 3 Look for the "Install" button in the address bar or click the menu
  4. 4 Select "Install [App Name]" to add it to your desktop
